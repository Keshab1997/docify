JobDoc is a document preparation app for adults. It resizes photos, prepares signatures, creates and merges PDFs, and can build a simple CV on your phone.

## Documents stay on your device
Photos, signatures, PDFs and text you type into a CV are processed locally on your phone. We do not upload those files to our server. We do not have an account system, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number inside the app.

## Ads
JobDoc shows advertisements through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), app interactions, diagnostic data, and IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location. This is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

JobDoc does not sell your documents. We do not have your documents on a server.

## Children
JobDoc is not directed to children under 13, and the target audience is 18+.

## Camera
If you choose to scan a page or capture a signature, the app uses the camera for that action only. Those images are handled on your device like any other photo you pick.
