App name: Docify: Photo , PDF & CV Maker

Short description (80 chars):
Resize photo, signature, PDF and CV for job and exam forms.

Full description:
Docify helps you prepare photos, signatures, PDFs and a simple CV for job applications, exam forms and college admissions.

Resize a photo to the exact KB limit. Make a passport-size photo. Clean a signature. Turn images into a PDF. Merge files. Keep everything on your phone.

What you can do
• Photo resize — min–max KB, exam presets (SSC, IBPS, Rail, UPSC), crop
• Passport size photo — 35×45 mm / 2×2 inch, white/blue/red background
• Signature — draw or clean a photo, then resize to the required KB
• Crop image
• JPG to PNG and PNG to JPG
• Image to PDF
• Merge PDF
• Compress PDF
• PDF to images
• Document scan — camera capture and crop, on your phone
• CV builder — simple local PDF, no account
• Job Form Assistant — photo, signature and PDF checklist for one application
• My Documents — files saved in the app, share with WhatsApp or any app

Built for online forms
Many portals reject a photo or signature because the size, pixels or format do not match. Docify is a preparation tool. It does not submit forms for you, and it is not an official app of any exam board or government.

Private by design
Photos, signatures, PDFs and CV text are processed on your device. Docify does not upload your documents to a server. The app shows ads through Google AdMob. See the privacy policy for what the ad SDK handles.

Docify is for adults preparing applications. It is not made for children.
