# Child Safety & Target Audience — Docify
Package: com.keshabstudios.docify
Last updated: 30 September 2026

## Target audience
- **Primary:** Adults 18+ preparing job applications, exam forms, college admissions
- **Secondary:** Students 16+ with parental guidance (where allowed by local law)

## Not directed to children
- Docify is NOT directed to children under 13
- Not a children's app per Play Families Policy
- No child-directed content, no cartoon characters, no games for children

## Content
- No sexual content, no violence, no gambling, no drugs
- No user-generated chat, no social features
- No location sharing
- No in-app purchases that could be abused by children

## Ads
- Contains ads via Google AdMob
- AdMob content rating set to G (General) — no mature ads
- No personalized ads directed to children (app is not child-directed)

## Data from children
- We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13
- If a parent believes child provided data via optional Google sign-in, email keshabsarkar2018@gmail.com for deletion

## Parental guidance
- If user is under 18, use with parental guidance
- Parents can enable fingerprint lock to prevent unauthorized access to My Documents
