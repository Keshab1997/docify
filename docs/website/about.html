WHY DOCIFY EXISTS

One wrong KB can cost you a form.

Online portals are picky. A photo that is 52 KB when the limit is 50, a signature in the wrong format, a PDF too heavy to upload — and the form bounces back.

Docify shows you the exact size, pixels and format before you save, so you fix it once and move on to the actual application.

✕ Photo is 52 KB — limit is 50 KB
✕ Signature must be a JPG, not a PNG
✕ PDF is too large to upload
✓ Docify: exact KB, pixels and format — ready
WHAT YOU CAN DO

Everything your application needs, in one app.

Pick a tool, set the rules your form asks for, save. That’s it.

Photo & passport photo

Set a minimum and maximum KB, pick an exam preset (SSC, IBPS, RRB, UPSC, PSC) or enter custom pixels. Make 35×45 mm or 2×2 inch passport photos on a white, blue or red background.

Signature

Draw it on screen or pick a photo of your signature, clean up the background, and resize it to exactly the KB your form asks for.

PDF tools

Turn several images into one PDF, merge PDFs, shrink a heavy file for upload, or pull pages out as pictures. There’s a document scanner, too.

CV builder

Ten professional templates — from Executive Navy to an Indian Bio-Data format for government, bank and PSC jobs. Exported as a clean PDF with no watermark.

HOW IT WORKS

Three taps from “rejected” to “accepted”.

Pick your file

Choose a photo or PDF from your gallery, or capture one with the camera. Nothing needs an account.

Set the rules

Enter the KB range, pixels and format from your form — or choose an exam preset and let Docify do the maths.

Save & share

Check the result, save it to My Documents or your gallery, and send it on through WhatsApp, Email, Drive or any app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your documents stay yours.

  • Photos, signatures, PDFs and CV text are processed on your device. Docify does not upload your documents — we don’t even run a server for them.
  • No login required. Every tool works as a guest. Signing in with Google is optional.
  • Optional backup goes to a Docify folder in your own Google Drive — your account, your storage. It’s off by default.
  • Lock My Documents with fingerprint, face or PIN. Android checks it; Docify never sees your biometrics.
  • The camera is used only when you tap Scan or Capture. No location, contacts or SMS.

To be upfront: Docify shows ads through Google AdMob, and that is its only income. There are no paid features. The internet is used only for ads and the optional Drive backup.

QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Is Docify free?

Yes. The app is supported by ads from Google AdMob, and there are no paid features or subscriptions.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Every tool works as a guest. Signing in with Google is only needed if you want the optional backup to your own Google Drive.

Are my photos and documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are processed on your phone and stored in the app’s own folder. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to back up to your own Google Drive.

Does my CV get a watermark?

No. CVs are exported as clean PDFs with no watermark and no account.

Does it work without internet?

The tools run on your device, so they work offline. The internet is only used to show ads and for the optional Drive backup.

Will my form be accepted?

Docify helps you match the size, pixels and format your form asks for, but each portal decides for itself. Check the official notification for the exact rules.
