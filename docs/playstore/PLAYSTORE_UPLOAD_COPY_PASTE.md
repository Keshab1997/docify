### Full description (copy full block)
```
Docify helps you prepare photos, signatures, PDFs and a simple CV for job applications, exam forms and college admissions — all on your phone, private by design.

Resize a photo to the exact KB limit. Make a passport-size photo. Clean a signature. Turn images into a PDF. Merge files. Compress PDF. Build a CV. Keep everything on your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Photo resize — set min–max KB, exam presets (SSC, IBPS, RRB Rail, UPSC, PSC), custom pixels, crop & compress
• Passport size photo — 35×45 mm / 2×2 inch, white/blue/red background, multiple copies in one sheet
• Signature resize — draw or pick photo, clean background, resize to required KB
• Crop image — free, square, 4:3, 16:9, passport ratios
• JPG to PNG and PNG to JPG — instant conversion
• Image to PDF — multiple images → one PDF
• Merge PDF — combine multiple PDFs
• Compress PDF — reduce size for upload
• PDF to images — extract pages as JPG
• Document scan — camera capture + auto crop, on your phone
• CV builder — 10 professional templates, simple local PDF, no account, no watermark
• Job Form Assistant — photo, signature and PDF checklist for one application
• My Documents — all files saved in app, share with WhatsApp, Email, Drive, or any app
• Fingerprint lock — protect My Documents with biometrics

BUILT FOR ONLINE FORMS
Many portals reject a photo or signature because size, pixels or format do not match. Docify is a preparation tool. It shows you the exact KB, pixels and format before you save. It does not submit forms for you, and it is not an official app of any exam board or government.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Photos, signatures, PDFs and CV text are processed ON YOUR DEVICE.
• Docify does NOT upload your documents to a server — we do not operate one.
• Optional backup: Sign in with Google → files go to a Docify folder in YOUR Google Drive — your account, your storage. Off by default.
• My Documents can be locked with fingerprint/face/PIN — Android checks it, we never see your biometrics.
• The app shows ads via Google AdMob. See privacy policy for ad SDK details.

FOR ADULTS PREPARING APPLICATIONS
Docify is for adults (18+) preparing job, exam and college forms. It is not made for children and is not directed to children under 13.

WHY DOCIFY?
• No login required — works fully as guest
• No watermark on CV (since v1.1.2)
• Exam presets for India: SSC CGL/CHSL, IBPS PO/Clerk, RRB, UPSC, WBCS, etc.
• Fast, offline, lightweight
• Made in India with care

PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED
• Internet — for ads and optional Drive backup
• Camera — only when you tap Scan / Capture signature
• No broad storage, no location, no contacts, no SMS

SUPPORT
Questions? Email keshabsarkar2018@gmail.com
Privacy: https://keshab1997.github.io/docify/privacy.html
Terms: https://keshab1997.github.io/docify/terms.html
Web preview: https://keshab1997.github.io/docify/preview/main/

Disclaimer: Docify is a preparation tool, not an official app of SSC, IBPS, RRB, UPSC, or any government. Check your form's official notification for exact photo/signature requirements.
```
